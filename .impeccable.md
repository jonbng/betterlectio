## Design Context

### Users

Danish students using BetterLectio throughout the school day. The feedback surface lets them quickly report a problem, suggest an improvement, or continue a conversation with BetterLectio's admins without leaving Lectio.

### Brand Personality

Clean, intuitive, and friendly. The product should feel capable and considered without becoming corporate, decorative, or visually separate from the rest of BetterLectio.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use BetterLectio's existing adaptive theme, color tokens, Geist typography, compact controls, and restrained surfaces. The feedback experience should resemble a polished conversation inbox: tall enough to read a thread comfortably, clear about who is speaking and what happens next, and equally coherent in light and dark themes.

### Design Principles

- Make the conversation the visual center of the experience.
- Keep actions obvious and language plain, especially around status and replies.
- Use space and hierarchy rather than extra cards or decoration.
- Match BetterLectio's existing tokens and theme choices.
- Preserve fast keyboard use, visible focus states, and practical mobile sizing.
